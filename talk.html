Talk icon

Add this to your phone's home screen. One tap opens Car mode and starts listening.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Keep the name Talk and tap Add.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep the name Talk and tap Add.

The first time, allow the microphone and location when asked. You stay signed in, as in the main app.
